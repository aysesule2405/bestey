I’m missing a tooth
my tongue shrivels to take up the space

I speak words I never heard my mother utter
she comes to me in the bluest hours, places a wet towel
on my forehead
she says
            you were built for war.

I reach for a sip of water
my shoulders crack with each turn
she says
            you were a quiet destruction
            when you were born.

one day, speech will be mine like other notions to be owned
like love
or superiority –
one foot on the throne, other in a bowl of blood
and short of breath, I will stop trying new colors on like the passing of seasons

in front of an art gallery it will happen
or at the piers
with a grin, I will come undone

            and break what’s yours, what’s mine
