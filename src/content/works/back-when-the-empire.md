I, strangely, love this figure of a girl standing under
an extravagant new year’s arch, dark lipstick and hands
crossed formally as if waiting in line for a visa interview.
she will lose half her heart to an empire that eats away
at lands of little might, she will bite each upper arm
she finds until someone bites her back. strangely, I
love, strangely, I clutch at my heart and complete the act,
weep folded in half. I want it fast-forwarded, I want it back.
the girl was a good wife with her hair dyed red, with the
unseasonly jacket you hate. alas, she stepped on her own
skirt and undressed herself halfway when the first fireworks
started going off. she got some help, made conversation
with the green birds, questioned if invasiveness was something
she should be worried about, like quicksand, or brown bears.
she answered her own question: no, no. fret not from love. love
strangely, looking back at an accordion of moments from bitter past
now distorted with many dreams of broken and hastily patched
places, love strangely, looking forward to the version of the girl
with longer hair, ferocious callouses, new best friends,
new ways of accepting touch. love strangely in january.
twist a wish as if raw pasta.
