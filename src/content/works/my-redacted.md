oh, please, you’re
my [redacted] –

wolf, weasel
what once was –

oh, please. you’re
tightening the leash again.

sorry about the canines, then
sorry about the corkscrews

and wet matches, never mind my youth
crushing and desolate.

water it, nothing grows
tend to it, the quiet tells you

a secret
that you can’t liken to a flower.

but, it all
began with rain.

the kind blond boy smiling
at two girls sharing an umbrella.

I whispered this silhouette of a desire
to the orange stands we walked by –

heart of coal,
make fire of me.

we speak not of the spark, but
oh, the thrill.

we’re perfect – jasmines
the size of our fists.
